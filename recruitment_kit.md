# Recruitment Kit (send tonight)

**Who qualifies:** their **first-ever** mutual fund investment was a SIP on **Groww**, started **3–15 months ago** (roughly Aug 2025 – Aug 2026). The SIP can be running, paused or stopped. People who stopped are especially useful.

## 4. Screener: Google Form (create at forms.google.com)

**Form title:** First SIP Experience: Research Chat Sign-up
**Description:** "For a student product-research project. We will never ask for PAN, account numbers, amounts or screenshots. Responses are used only to schedule a chat."

| # | Question | Type | Options |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | First name | Short answer | |

| 3 | Was your first SIP your **first-ever** mutual fund investment? | Multiple choice | Yes / No / Not sure |
| 4 | Which app did you start it on? | Multiple choice | Groww / Zerodha / INDmoney / Paytm Money / Other |

**Qualify if:** Q3 = Yes, Q4 = Groww, Q5 = 3–15 months.
**Prioritise:** a mix of Q6/Q7 answers, so you get some who paused or stopped and some who continued.
**After Gate 1 (payment failures), prioritise:**
- ~3 people with Q10b = "Yes…" or "Not sure" (a failed or missed instalment), especially "found out later"
- ~2–3 whose SIP ran smoothly (Q10b = "No"), for contrast
- Within the segment: Q8 = 21–28 and Q9 = first job / student (stipend)

*Don't mention payment failures in the WhatsApp message itself. Keep it about "your first SIP experience" so you don't attract or prime only people with complaints.*

- Post in **5+ groups tonight**. Response rates are low on a Friday night, so volume matters.

- Ask 2–3 friends to forward it in their office or college groups.
- **Cutoff:** check the sheet Sat 12 PM. If you have fewer than 6 qualified people, broaden Q4 to any app (and note it as a limitation).
