## What this is about
A student project about **first-time SIP investors on Groww**. We want to understand **why SIP payments fail or stop in the first year**, even when people still want to invest.

You will do two things:
1. **Run a short online survey.** It puts numbers on the problem.
2. **Run 5–6 short interviews** (15 minutes each). They tell us the *why*.

## Part B: The interviews (~20 minutes each, including notes)

### Who to interview
- **Must:** their **first-ever SIP was on Groww**, started **3 to 15 months ago**.
- **Try to get this mix of 5–6 people:**
  - about **3** who had a failed or missed SIP payment, or who paused or stopped their SIP
  - about **2–3** whose SIP has run smoothly
- If someone doesn't fit the "must" rule, thank them politely and don't count them.

### For each interview
1. **Book 15 minutes** on a call (Google Meet or phone), in Hindi or English, whichever they prefer.
2. Give them a code: **P1, P2, P3…** in the order you interview them. **Never write their real name in the notes.**
3. Open `step7_interview_kit.md` and **follow it from top to bottom.** It tells you exactly what to say.
4. In Step 3, share your screen with `step7_concept_cards.md`. **Use the order shown in the script for that person's code**, e.g. P2 sees B → C → A.
5. At the end, ask if they'd try a 15-minute prototype on Sunday night or Monday morning. Note yes/no and a time.
6. **Straight after the call**, fill in the notes table at the bottom of the script while it's fresh. Copy the table once per person.

### The rules (very important)
- ✅ **Read the opening line** that explains the project and asks permission to take notes.
- ✅ **Write their exact words** for good quotes, even in Hindi or Hinglish. Everything else can be in English.
- ✅ **If they don't want to answer something, move on.**
- ❌ **Never ask** for PAN, account numbers, amounts invested, or screenshots.
- ❌ **Never give advice**, even if they ask ("Should I stop my SIP?"). Say: *"I'm only here to learn, sorry I can't advise."*
- ❌ **Don't suggest answers.** Ask "What happened?", not "Was the app at fault?"
- ❌ **Don't say which of the 3 ideas you like.** Just listen.
- ❌ **Don't record the call** unless they clearly say yes.

### If the call is running long
Keep **Q2, Q3, Q4 and the 3 ideas**, because these matter most. Skip **Q5 and Q6** first.

### Common situations
| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| Their SIP never had a payment problem | That's fine and useful. In Q3, ask the "If NO" question. |
| They ask what you think | "I'm only here to learn, your view matters more!" |
| They ask for investment advice | "Sorry, I can't advise. A SEBI-registered adviser can help." |
| They get emotional or upset about money | Be kind, don't push, and move to the next question or end early. |
| They started their SIP on another app | Thank them and end politely. Don't count them. |
