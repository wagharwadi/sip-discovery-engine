# Concept cards (share your screen at the end of the interview)

*~3 minutes. Show all three, in a different order for each person (P1: A-B-C, P2: B-C-A, P3: C-A-B, …). Read each one aloud.*
*For each, ask: "When would this have helped you, if ever?" and "Help or pressure?" Then: "Only one: which, and why?" Don't say which one you like.*

---

## Idea A: "My payday SIP"

When you set up a SIP (or anytime later), you tell the app **in your own words** when your money usually comes in.
*e.g. "salary on the 1st", "stipend around the 7th, sometimes late"*

The app suggests a SIP date **a day or two after** your money arrives, and shows exactly **which day money will leave your account**.
You choose: **move my date** or **keep it as it is**.

---

## Idea B: "When an instalment fails"

If a SIP payment doesn't go through, you get **one clear message**:

- **What happened**, in plain words:
  *"Your bank declined the payment on the 4th because the balance was low. The debit runs one day before your SIP date."*
- **What happens next:**
  *"Nothing is lost. Your SIP continues next month. If 3 payments in a row fail, the SIP stops."*
- **One tap to choose:** retry now · change my date · skip this month · do nothing

---

## Idea C: "Skip, not stop"

When you open **Pause** or **Stop**, the app asks one optional question: *"What's going on?"* You can type anything, or skip the question.

If it sounds like a **short-term money gap**, the app also shows **"Skip just this month"**, side by side with Pause and Stop.
**All three are equally easy.** Nothing is hidden, and no one tries to talk you out of stopping.

---

### And: none of these?
*"If none of these would have helped you, what would have?"*
