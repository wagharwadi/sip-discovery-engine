# Interview Script (15 minutes)

Read this during the call. Ask the questions in order.

---

## Before the call

- [ ] The person's **first SIP is on Groww** and started **3 to 15 months ago**
- [ ] The 3 idea cards are open, ready to share: `step7_concept_cards.md`
- [ ] The notes table (at the bottom of this page) is open
- [ ] Their name is replaced with a code: **P1, P2, P3…**

---

## Remember during the call

1. **Don't suggest answers.** Ask "What happened?", not "Was it the app's fault?"
2. **Wait 3 seconds** after they answer. They often add something important.
3. **Never give advice**, even if they ask. Say: "I'm only here to learn."
4. **Never ask** for PAN, account numbers, amounts or screenshots.

---

## Step 1: Start (30 seconds)

Say:

> "Thanks for your time! This is a 15-minute chat for my student project about first SIPs.
> There are no right or wrong answers. I won't ask for your PAN, account details or amounts.
> Is it okay if I take notes?"

---

## Step 2: Ask these 7 questions (12 minutes)

**Q1. Why did you start your first SIP? What did you expect from it?**

**Q2. When does your money usually come in each month? And what is your SIP date?**
- Follow-up: "Did you choose that date yourself?"

**Q3. Has a SIP payment ever failed, bounced or not gone through?**

- **If YES**, ask all four:
  - a. "How did you find out? How long after?"
  - b. "What do you think caused it?"
  - c. "What did you do next? What happened to the SIP after that?"
  - d. "Did it change how you feel about the SIP or the app?"
- **If NO**, ask:
  - "How do you make sure the money is there on SIP day?"

**Q4. Have you ever paused, skipped, reduced or thought about stopping your SIP? What was going on then?**

**Q5. What do you look at in the app, how often, and how does it make you feel?**

**Q6. When you're unsure about your SIP, who or what do you check with?**

**Q7. What would make you more likely to keep your SIP going?**

---

## Step 3: Show the 3 ideas (3 minutes)

1. Share your screen with `step7_concept_cards.md`.
2. Change the order for each person:

   | Person | Order to show |
   |---|---|
   | P1, P4 | A → B → C |
   | P2, P5 | B → C → A |
   | P3, P6 | C → A → B |

3. For **each** idea, ask:
   - "When would this have helped **you**, if ever?"
   - "Does it feel like help, or like pressure?"
4. Then ask: **"If you could have only one, which one would you pick? Why?"**

Don't say which idea you like.

---

## Step 4: End (30 seconds)

Say:

> "Thank you, this really helps! Could you spare 15 minutes on Sunday night or Monday morning to try a small prototype?"

Write down their answer.
