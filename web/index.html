In our interviews, every failed SIP payment had one cause: the money came after the SIP date. Rhythm SIP sets the date from your payday, checks in before each payment, and bends when a month runs late.

      A tough month doesn't end your SIP

      Step 6 · Over time

      It learns your payday from your taps, not your bank

      Every "money's in" or "yes" is one data point. When three months line up, Rhythm offers to lock the date and stop asking. No bank data needed.

      Ask anything about SIP payments
      • All money is simulated. No bank data is read and nothing is stored.
      • Calendar maths counts weekends but ignores bank holidays.
      • Phase 1 (check-in, moving a month, Lifelines) runs on Groww's existing date-change and skip features. The "invest the day after you tap" mode needs Phase 2 rails (on-demand UPI AutoPay), still to be validated.
      • Bank charges are illustrative: ₹500 + 18% GST per failed auto-debit; banks charge ₹250–750. Groww itself charges nothing.
